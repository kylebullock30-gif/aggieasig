Our brotherhood

Built on character.

Through brotherhood, service, and the everyday work of showing up for one another, we put a simple idea into practice: To Better the Man.

03

Since 1845

A tradition that continues.

Alpha Sigma Phi was founded at Yale University on December 6, 1845. At Texas A&M, Theta Rho carries that brotherhood into our own campus community.
